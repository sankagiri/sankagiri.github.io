A faculty talk at the Wadhwani School of Data Science and AI, IIT Madras, on September 24, 2026. I discuss how human comparisons can teach machines to represent similarity, and why fixed distances between embeddings do not fully explain our choices: similarity judgments depend on the alternatives presented. The talk explores probabilistic clustering models as a way to capture these context effects, along with preliminary results and ongoing work on learning embeddings efficiently from similarity choices.
