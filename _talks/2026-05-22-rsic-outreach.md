An outreach talk for students at Research Science Initiative-Chennai on May 22, 2026. Using video recommendations as a running example, I introduce the exploration–exploitation tradeoff: when should we try something new, and when should we stick with what seems best? The talk develops the multi-armed bandit framework, confidence intervals, and the upper confidence bound (UCB) algorithm, before connecting these ideas to my research on learning from comparisons.
